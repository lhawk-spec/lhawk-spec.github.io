Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who uses this app

OMR Brain is used only by its owner, Luke Hawkins, with his own Google accounts.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads Gmail messages and their labels, and may add or remove labels and archive messages in the owner's own mailboxes. It does not send or permanently delete email.

How the data is handled

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
